# Hey, I'm Brandon! 👋

I'm Brandon Carmona Guerena, a high school student in Phoenix, Arizona, with a passion for robotics, electronics, and engineering.

I enjoy understanding how things work, building projects from scratch, and learning through experimentation—even when things don't go as planned.

## 🤖 Featured Project: Bloopy

[Bloopy](https://github.com/BrandonC27-Engineer/Bloopy-Robot) is my personal AI companion robot project.

I designed and built a stationary prototype combining:

- Custom PCB designs and a 3D-printed stand
- ESP32-based sensors and OLED eye animations
- Raspberry Pi processing, camera, microphone, and speaker integration
- An AI-assisted companion app for interacting with the robot

I used AI tools to help develop parts of the software while directing the design, assembling the hardware, integrating components, and troubleshooting the system.

**[Explore the Bloopy project →](https://github.com/BrandonC27-Engineer/Bloopy-Robot)**

## 🔧 My Engineering Journey

My interest in electronics grew through Arduino courses and hands-on projects.

After learning basic circuits and programming, I built an Arduino Uno R3 project integrating an ultrasonic distance sensor, LCD display, and keypad.

I also experimented with connecting multiple sensors to an Arduino Mega. Although that project was unsuccessful because of soldering and connection difficulties, it motivated me to improve my electronics assembly skills.

## 🚀 What I'm Working Toward

My long-term goal is to develop robots that make technology feel more personal, interactive, and accessible.

I'm especially interested in combining mechanical design, electronics, embedded systems, and artificial intelligence into complete robotic products.

I believe some of the most valuable engineering lessons come from building something, discovering why it doesn't work, and trying again.
